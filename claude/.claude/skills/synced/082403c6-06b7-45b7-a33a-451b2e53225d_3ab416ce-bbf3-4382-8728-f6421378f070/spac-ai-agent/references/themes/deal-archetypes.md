# Deal Archetypes — Cross-Name Pattern Recognition

## Core idea

Some SPAC deals match a **multi-trait archetype** that is more specific and prescriptive than the sponsor archetype alone. Sponsor archetype answers *"what kind of sponsor is this?"* Deal archetype answers *"what kind of deal does this combination of sponsor + UW + target + structure produce, and what's the playbook?"*

When a deal hits ALL of the trigger conditions for a recognized archetype, the playbook is determined — apply it without re-deriving from first principles. The sponsor archetype is the starting point; the deal archetype is the resolved combination once the target is announced and the BCA is on file.

**When the skill consults this file:** Step 4 (after Sponsor Archetype Assignment), and Step 5 (auto-fire screens) when the archetype dictates a specific Meteora seat (FPA, NRA, ELOC, structured paper). If a recognized archetype matches, name it explicitly in the verdict block and apply the playbook in the recommendation.

**Extensibility:** new archetypes are added as additional `## Archetype: …` sections below. The list will grow as Meteora encounters more cross-name patterns. Each archetype must specify (1) trigger conditions, all of which must hit, (2) the playbook when matched, (3) reference cases.

## Archetype: Asian-shell + low-quality-UW + banker-shopped target

### Trigger conditions (must hit ALL)

1. **Low-quality UW.** D. Boral, Maxim, AGP, Clear Street, I-Bankers cohort. Co-managers from the same low-quality cohort reinforce the signal.
2. **Asian sponsor with <$100M trust.** Taiwan / HK / Singapore / mainland-affiliated principals, BVI or Cayman sponsor entity. Small trust (~$50–80M) is part of the pattern — these are not large institutional vehicles.
3. **Target outside the SPAC's stated geographic mandate.** Asian-focused sponsor → European / North American / other non-Asian target, or vice versa. The mandate-mismatch is the banker-shopped tell.
4. **No committed PIPE + ELOC permission language in BCA.** Covenant uses "reasonable best efforts" for PIPE Investment "and/or" ELOC, with explicit language that there is no present requirement to pursue either. The ELOC mention alongside PIPE telegraphs structured paper expectations.
5. **All-stock 100% rollover.** No cash, no preferred, no convertible notes, no SAFEs in target consideration. Existing target shareholders roll 100% via Parent.
6. **Class B super-voting structure for founder.** >5 votes/share (often 10–15), par-value-only economics, non-transferable, issued for nominal consideration outside the headline consideration cap. Founder takes permanent voting control.
7. **Earnout pegged disproportionately to headline valuation.** Revenue or EBITDA trigger at a level that mathematically implies current scale is a small fraction of the headline number (e.g., $7M FY27 revenue trigger on $250M headline = revenue grows several-fold to hit; current revenue likely well below trigger). The earnout is the parties admitting the headline is decoupled from current scale.

### Playbook when archetype matches

- **Deal closure:** LIKELY closes mechanically because there's no MCC, no debt-CoC trap, structure built for full redemptions. Hedge on F-4 friction when FPI / foreign-target / Asian-sponsor combine — these slip but rarely break. Use **"likely closes"**, not "mechanically closes" — preserve the calibration.
- **Post-close trading:** **$1–4 base case** following the Asian-shell analog set (GTEC, MLGO, HOLO, UK, LBPS). Median outcome: $10 IPO → $1–3 within 18 months post-close.
- **Meteora seat:** **The FPA / structured-paper seat is the only real trade.** Common and warrants are noise EXCEPT at specific build triggers — typically **6%+ IRR on common to estimated close**, **sub-10c on warrants as a flyer**. Specific thresholds depend on the deal; pull from the name-specific memory doc if it exists. Do NOT fabricate thresholds.
- **Build-to-13G discipline:** if and only if common produces the IRR threshold via opportunistic sub-trust prints, accumulate toward the largest-13G holder threshold (9.9% common O/S). Below threshold yield, no build.
- **Sponsor pitch:** approach sponsor (typically through the IPO underwriter relationship) and target's exclusive financial advisor with structured-paper proposal. Standard FPA terms (spread to trust + reset on breakage + reset on trust interest), duration matching outside date. Given target quality, price aggressively — require founder-share kickdown or warrant package on top of customary FPA economics. If Meteora participated in the IPO at relationship-driven sizing, that allocation is leverage for the FPA pitch.

### Reference cases

- **MMTX / CADV** (4/2026, Polish IT helpdesk, D. Boral UW, Taiwanese sponsor via BVI, Class B 15-vote, $7M FY27 earnout on $250M headline)
- **FSHP / Great Future Tech** (Asian-shell, multiple target switches, ~56% redemption already at Aug 2025 vote, deadline pushes)
- **Ucommune (UK)**, **MicroAlgo (MLGO)**, **MicroCloud (HOLO)**, **Greenland (GTEC)** — historical analog set; all Asian-shell consultancy chain (Zhong / Hony / banker network), all traded $10 → $1–3 within 18 months post-close

### FPI gradation (sub-rule)

Polish / European / Israeli targets produce **less F-4 friction** than Chinese / PRC targets:
- No AFCRA equivalent
- No PRC-specific PCAOB inspection regime
- No CFIUS overhang for non-China FPI

But a foreign founder-funded entity with no institutional VC and statutory-only local accounts still produces SEC comment cycles, restatements, and timeline slippage. **Polish > Chinese ≠ clean.** Don't collapse this distinction in the writeup.

### Contextual bucket adjustment (Vik 4/27/26 framework)

The DNA of an Asian-shell + low-quality-UW + banker-shopped sponsor is constant across vintages, but the structure they get to issue varies with the IPO market:

- **Oct 2025 vintage:** these sponsors got out at 1 full warrant + 18mo + 3 paid extensions
- **Apr 2026 vintage:** equivalent low-quality DNA gets 1/4 right or 1/5 right + similar timeline
- If a "full warrant" sponsor would have been forced into 1/4 right today, the full warrant is **not** a quality signal — it's a relic of a closed window

When grading historical SPACs, ask: *"what would this same sponsor team have gotten in today's market?"* — not just *"what unit terms did they get?"* Same DNA, different paper, by vintage.
