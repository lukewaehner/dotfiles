## Digital Asset Treasury (DAT) / crypto treasury vehicles

**Status: DEAD.** 

- Premium compression across the 2025 DAT cohort; NAV discounts persisted contrary to arbitrage expectations
- MBAV/ReserveOne, SBET/SharpLink, BTBT, EMPD, DFDV, FORD all experienced severe decline from entry premiums to structural discounts
- DATs became correlated fundamental exposures rather than arbitrage trades
- New pre-deal SPACs targeting a DAT structure are an automatic failure signal — retail appetite exhausted, no investor base
- Do NOT suggest a pre-deal SPAC will "likely" announce a DAT target regardless of sponsor identity

**If a SPAC announces a DAT-structured deal in 2026**: the thesis is "redeem at trust, short the post-close common, do not own above trust at any point."

## "Adjusted net debt" treating inventory as cash

**Status: permanently dead as an accepted metric.**

- When management reclassifies working capital as debt-reduction to make leverage look manageable, use gross debt only
- Trasteel / SZZL is the live reference — $525M gross debt reframed as "$150M adjusted net debt"; real leverage is 22x Swiss GAAP EBITDA

## Retail-meme-only post-close names >6 months old

**Status: DEAD as a carry trade.**

- Names that re-rated post-close purely on retail momentum (no fundamental progression) have mean-reverted below trust-equivalent within 6–12 months
- Do NOT hold post-close equity on a pure "sponsor did a meme deal" thesis past announcement pop
- Exit into the pop, do not ride the re-rate
