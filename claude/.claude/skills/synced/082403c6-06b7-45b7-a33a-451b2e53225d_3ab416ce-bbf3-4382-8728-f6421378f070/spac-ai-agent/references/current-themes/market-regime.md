# SPAC Market Regime — Q2 2026

*Last updated: 2026-04-23*

## Flow imbalance

- ~30 SPAC IPOs per month entering the market
- ~2–3 deal announcements per month clearing
- Resulting backlog: a structural oversupply of pre-deal shells hunting for targets

## Outcome base rate

- 30–70% of announced SPAC deals either liquidate or close with sub-$1 / insolvent target businesses
- <10% of deal announcements are investable above-trust opportunities
- The skeptical prior: every new deal announcement starts at "likely to trade below trust post-close" until proven otherwise

## Retail-correlation regime

- Q4 2025 was a crowded retail-momentum complex across SPACs, SPAC warrants, and structured DAT exposures — correlations compressed toward one
- Regime broke in Q1 2026 — retail flows no longer reliably rescue bad deals post-close
- Current: fundamentals-first evaluation, retail-memeability is necessary-but-not-sufficient

## PIPE capital environment

- PIPE capital scarce across the universe
- "Reasonable best efforts" language at BCA signing = placeholder, not committed
- Deals that require plain-$10 equity PIPE to close are structurally impaired
- Alternative structures active: FPAs, ELOCs, non-redemption agreements, convertible preferred, structured converts — Meteora origination opportunity set

## Sponsor sale / shell-trade activity

- Mid-lifecycle sponsor transfers (shell sold to a new sponsor group before deal announcement) have become a live sub-pattern
- Typical dynamics: purchase price in the low-to-mid $8M range, negotiated reduction of deferred UW with revert-to-full-amount poison pill, new sponsor absorbs Admin Services obligations
- Read: when a sponsor pays meaningful cash + takes on committed deferred UW obligations, deal is lined up; mechanic is then to preserve trust through cheap no-contribution extensions until announcement

## Extension-meeting postponement mechanic

- Confirmed cadence: sponsor can serially postpone a noticed extension meeting via 8-K, shifting the redemption deadline forward ~8 days at a time
- No trust contribution required, no new redemption window opened, no new proxy / record date
- Capped only by the hard liquidation deadline
- Pattern of multiple postponements within a short window = sponsor stalling for deal announcement before letting holders vote/redeem
- Live reference example: SIMA (Apr 2026, Dominari)

## Implications for Meteora positioning

- Half of the book appropriate in redeemable pre-deal common from top-archetype sponsors trading below trust
- Half of the book in structured capital products originated into deals that can't close on plain equity
- On every deal announcement: build dialogue with sponsor/target, accumulate toward top-5 13G/13F visibility, offer FPA or structured capital as preferred counterparty
