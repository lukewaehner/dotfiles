# Live Themes — Q2 2026

*Last updated: 2026-04-23*

Categories where retail-memeability is currently earning post-close, and structural features that align with above-trust outcomes. These are the filters to apply when evaluating a new target announcement or handicapping a pre-deal sponsor's likely pipeline.

**Important**: "live theme" does not mean "buy automatically." It means "the base rate of above-trust outcomes is higher than the general SPAC universe if the sponsor can actually distribute." Deal-specific execution still dominates.

---

## Trump-adjacent defense, critical minerals, rare earths

- CRML (Critical Minerals) = reference post-close re-rate driven by Greenland + Trump + DOD stockpile policy
- Sponsor-level: proximity to Trump orbit matters (DOMH, Dominari ecosystem, American Bitcoin)
- Target profile that clears: US-domiciled, tied to a named policy initiative, concrete DOD/government customer or pipeline
- What does NOT clear: "critical minerals" as a theme without a specific US-policy catalyst

## AI infrastructure with real revenue

- Must have actual deployed revenue, not LOIs and pipeline
- $10M+ real institutional VC rounds in target cap table = PCAOB audit readiness proxy
- Target profile that clears: US-domiciled, operating, infrastructure-tier (not wrapper-around-API)
- What does NOT clear: "AI strategy" slides without deployed product

## Enhanced / regulated-vice gaming

- APAD (Enhanced Games) is the reference — multi-sport live event with structural catalyst date (Games May 2026), live revenue progression, named athlete signings
- Retail-memeability comes from the event calendar being visible in advance
- Pattern: hard external event deadline creates proxy-timeline compression that the market prices into rights

## Bank-franchise retail distribution of memeable targets

- Dominari, Cantor (Brandon Lutnick), Cohen (Columbus Circle) as sponsor class is a live top-tier archetype for SPACs specifically
- SPACs are retail-momentum venture dumps; bank-franchise distribution capability matters more than operating pedigree for this product class
- This is the archetype most likely to successfully pair a memeable target with the retail demand to absorb post-close float

## Structured capital on below-trust common + rights < 30c + no PIPE

- This combination flags a Meteora origination opportunity
- FPA, ELOC, convert, or non-redemption agreement fits where plain-$10 equity PIPE will not clear
- Pattern: build to top-5 13G/13F holder on announcement, approach sponsor as preferred counterparty

## Serial hype-deal franchise execution

- Lunn & Cannon (Archimedes franchise), Harry You (dMY, Berto) — repeat execution on getting retail-memeable deals to tape
- Track record is "gets deals to tape that trade" (APAD template), not "picks long-term winners"
- Trade is typically: long on deal announcement, exit into the first meaningful pop, do not ride through 6-month-post-close mean reversion

## Event-deadline compression as driver

- Any target with a hard external event (games, regulatory window, product launch window, policy deadline) before the outside date creates a proxy-timeline pressure that the rights market prices
- APAD + Enhanced Games is the clearest current example

## Protocol when semantic search returns live-theme context

1. Treat as a live-regime signal, not stale content
2. Cross-check the specific deal at hand against the theme's criteria (not just the category)
3. If the specific deal is within the theme category but fails the structural criteria (e.g., "AI infrastructure" with no real revenue), flag that the theme does NOT apply to this deal
