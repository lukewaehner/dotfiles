## Credible operator / toxic-shell middle-tier

### Avi Katz / GigCapital franchise

- Real C-suite operator, serial SPAC sponsor
- Structural problem: raises toxic dilutive shells with onerous dilution (rights, founder shares, warrant structure), will not forfeit
- Only attracts dilution-insensitive targets — science-project companies at fantasy valuations
- Active example: GIG (GigCapital7) + Hadron Energy (nuclear, led by kid Samuel Gibson with no nuclear expertise, $4.9B valuation)
- Pattern: treat as middle-tier regardless of sponsor pedigree. Real operator does not overcome structural toxicity.

## Banker-shopped first-timer (default bottom half of universe)

Pattern — not named franchises, a general category:

- First-time sponsor, no prior SPAC
- No domain fit with target if one is announced
- UW shopped the deal, sponsor did not source
- 1/4 right or smaller unit structure
- Most 2021–2025 retail SPAC cohort falls here
- Examples: Sizzle II / Trasteel (restaurateurs + footwear CEO → European steel trader), IRHO / Electra (Bengochea ex-Skadden junior lawyer → Boston AI-BMS)

## Protocol for new-sponsor identification

When a new SPAC surfaces whose sponsor is not in this file:

1. Do not guess the archetype — extract from the S-1 / 14F-1 / 10-K
2. If the sponsor has a prior SPAC, pull that deal's post-close trajectory before assigning tier
3. If the sponsor is a bank / broker-dealer, check their DeSPAC league table participation over the prior 12 months — volume matters for distribution capability
4. If the sponsor team is first-time and 2-person, default to banker-shopped first-timer archetype until evidence otherwise
