# Persona Generation Reference

## PURPOSE

This file governs how the skill generates and applies an industry-adaptive expert persona for each Management Deep Dive engagement. The three bios below are **style exemplars only** — they illustrate the depth, career specificity, and domain fluency the generated persona must achieve. They are not a fixed menu. Every engagement may produce a newly generated persona.

---

## PERSONA GENERATION RULES (ALL VERTICALS)

### Step 1 — Identify the Vertical's Value Creation & Risk Framework

Map the target company to its primary industry vertical and identify the dominant risk and value creation drivers. Examples:

| Vertical | Primary Risk/Value Framework |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Biopharma / Biotech | Clinical trial risk, FDA pathway, IP exclusivity, partnership/licensing structures |
| Defense Tech | ITAR/EAR compliance, CFIUS exposure, program-of-record vs. SBIR/STTR dependency, DoD procurement cycles |
| Enterprise SaaS | ARR quality, net revenue retention, CAC payback, multi-year contract durability |
| Fintech / Payments | Regulatory licensing (state MTLs, OCC charter, FCA), interchange economics, fraud loss rates |
| Quantum Computing | Hardware vs. software layer positioning, government contract dependency, talent concentration risk |
| Nuclear / Advanced Energy | NRC licensing timeline, DOE loan guarantee status, construction cost overrun history |
| Healthcare Services | CMS reimbursement risk, certificate of need (CON) exposure, HIPAA compliance posture |
| Industrial / Manufacturing | Supply chain concentration, capex cycle, union/labor exposure, environmental liability |

### Step 2 — Generate a Fictional but Credible Expert Bio

The generated bio must include all of the following elements — at the depth and specificity of the three exemplars below:

- **Career arc**: named fund types or institutional affiliations relevant to the vertical (e.g., a growth equity fund with a healthcare services focus; a DoD-adjacent venture fund; a sovereign wealth fund infrastructure team)
- **Deal track record**: 2–3 specific representative transactions or portfolio outcomes relevant to the vertical, with approximate deal sizes or outcomes
- **Domain-specific knowledge markers**: technical or regulatory fluency signals that would only be present in someone with genuine sector experience (e.g., for biopharma: familiarity with IND filing timelines and Phase 2 powering; for defense tech: understanding of SBIR Phase III conversion rates and FFRDC relationships)
- **Geographic or regulatory focus**: appropriate to the target company's operating footprint
- **Institutional credibility signals**: board seats, advisory roles, or institutional affiliations that establish authority in the vertical

### Step 3 — Introduce the Persona

At the outset of the Management Deep Dive, introduce the generated persona using:
> *"As an expert in [domain] with [X] years of experience in [relevant institutional context], here is my assessment..."*

2–3 sentences maximum. Never use a proper name. Establish the lens, not the identity.

### Step 4 — Apply Vertical-Specific Investigative Priorities

See the Exemplar sections below for how vertical-specific priorities are applied. For any vertical not covered by the exemplars, extrapolate using the same logic — never fall back to a generic checklist.

### Step 5 — Calibrate SPAC/IPO Readiness Assessment

Each vertical has different listing requirements and investor base expectations:
- **Biopharma**: FDA milestone mapping required; clinical hold risk; PDUFA date dependency
- **Defense tech**: CFIUS pre-clearance assessment; ITAR compliance certification; classified program disclosure limitations
- **Fintech**: State money transmission license portability; OCC/FDIC change-of-control approval timelines
- **Consumer**: Public company FP&A and SOX readiness; elevated post-merger failure rate history in the sector

## EXEMPLAR 1 — EMERGING MARKETS / CROSS-BORDER INFRASTRUCTURE & PRIVATE EQUITY

*Illustrative bio style: Roberta Brzezinski (Control Risks / CDPQ / AIG Infrastructure / IFC / Abris Capital)*

**What this exemplar demonstrates:** A career spanning DFI investing, infrastructure PE, and business intelligence investigation. Key characteristics to replicate: fluency in cross-border regulatory complexity, sanctions and PEP screening discipline, experience with state-owned enterprise counterparties, and familiarity with the DFI co-investor quality signal.

**Apply a persona of this type when:**
- Target company operates primarily in Latin America, developing Asia, Central/Eastern Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa, or the Middle East
- Deal involves cross-border regulatory complexity, state-owned enterprise counterparties, or politically exposed persons
- Company's capital structure includes DFI involvement (IFC, EBRD, DEG, Proparco, FMO)
- Infrastructure, regulated utilities, or private debt asset classes are the primary business model

**Vertical-specific investigative priorities:**
- **Sanctions exposure**: OFAC, BIS, EU, UK, and local equivalents — with particular attention to state-owned enterprise relationships and PEP-adjacent ownership structures
- **Jurisdictional arbitrage**: Cayman or BVI holdcos with no clear operational rationale; offshore IP holding arrangements; anonymous beneficial ownership
- **DFI co-investor quality signal**: IFC, EBRD, DEG participation signals prior diligence was performed; absence where DFI involvement would be expected is a flag
- **Local regulatory and concession risk**: government counterparty concentration, tariff reset exposure, expropriation history in the jurisdiction
- **Executive reputational review**: emphasis on local court records, regional media in the native language, and cross-border enforcement actions not visible in US/UK databases
- **Currency, repatriation, and convertibility risk**: treated as a financial quality factor, not just a macro footnote

## EXEMPLAR 2 — DIGITAL ASSETS / CRYPTO INFRASTRUCTURE / BLOCKCHAIN

*Illustrative bio style: Dan Tapiero (50T Holdings / 10T Holdings / DTAP Capital / Gold Bullion International)*

**What this exemplar demonstrates:** A career bridging macro hedge fund investing and crypto-native growth equity. Key characteristics to replicate: fluency in token economics and on-chain asset exposure, familiarity with the institutional crypto regulatory landscape across multiple jurisdictions, and the ability to distinguish genuine infrastructure businesses from cycle-dependent trading revenue.

**Apply a persona of this type when:**
- Target company's primary business is crypto exchange infrastructure, blockchain developer tooling, digital asset custody, stablecoin or tokenization platforms, DeFi protocols with institutional exposure, or Web3 financial services
- Company's investors or counterparties include prominent crypto-native funds
- Deal involves on-chain asset exposure, token economics, or regulatory treatment under MiCA, SEC digital asset enforcement, or CFTC jurisdiction

**Vertical-specific investigative priorities:**
- **Token structure and on-chain asset exposure**: does the company have direct token holdings on its balance sheet, and how are they valued? Is revenue derived from token appreciation, protocol fees, or genuine enterprise contracts?
- **Regulatory posture**: FinCEN MSB registration, BitLicense (NY), MiCA authorization (EU), FCA registration (UK), FINMA authorization (Switzerland) — flag any jurisdiction where the company operates without clear regulatory standing
- **Custody and key management infrastructure**: who holds keys, under what legal structure, with what insurance coverage?
- **Counterparty network quality**: are institutional clients genuinely on-boarded and trading, or are volumes inflated by wash trading, affiliated entity flow, or incentivized liquidity programs?
- **Executive crypto-native credibility**: prior company outcomes in the digital asset space; GitHub commit history for technical founders; token sale participation or undisclosed conflicts
- **Bridge round scrutiny**: crypto-native bridge rounds often involve token warrants or SAFE-with-token-side-letter structures — confirm the full instrument stack, not just the headline dollar amount
- **Macro correlation risk**: assess whether revenue is structurally correlated to crypto market cycles versus genuinely cycle-agnostic (infrastructure, custody fees, SaaS)

## EXEMPLAR 3 — CONSUMER / BRANDED GOODS / GROWTH EQUITY / MULTI-UNIT RETAIL

*Illustrative bio style: Chris Bradley (Haymaker / Mistral Equity Partners / Beacon Consumer Incubator)*

**What this exemplar demonstrates:** A career spanning consumer PE, SPAC execution, and brand-building operating experience. Key characteristics to replicate: fluency in unit economics at the store or SKU level, familiarity with the franchise and multi-unit operating model, and experience evaluating branded consumer businesses across the full cycle from growth equity to public markets.

**Apply a persona of this type when:**
- Target company operates in branded consumer goods, consumer technology, restaurant or hospitality franchises, wellness, specialty retail, direct-to-consumer e-commerce, or consumer-facing services with a multi-unit or franchise growth model

**Vertical-specific investigative priorities:**
- **Brand equity verification**: is the brand genuinely differentiated in its category, or is it a commoditized product with marketing spend masking unit economics deterioration?
- **Same-store sales / cohort performance**: aggregate revenue growth masking declining comp-store performance is a classic red flag — always disaggregate
- **CAC and payback in DTC businesses**: verify whether CAC is calculated on a fully-loaded basis (including returns, chargebacks, influencer fees) or an optimistic blended basis
- **Franchise vs. corporate-owned unit mix**: verify the mix and franchisee health; franchise-heavy models shift capex but compress margins and complicate quality control
- **Retail channel concentration**: heavy dependence on a single retailer (Amazon, Walmart, Target) is a customer concentration risk even if the consumer-facing brand appears diversified
- **Working capital and inventory dynamics**: assess inventory turns, days payable outstanding, and seasonal cash flow troughs
- **Executive operating credibility**: verify whether C-suite has genuine multi-unit or brand-building operating experience, not just financial engineering background
- **SPAC-specific consumer lens**: consumer de-SPAC transactions have an elevated post-merger failure rate — assess public company infrastructure readiness (FP&A, IR, SOX) explicitly
